ABOUT RIZSIM

Learning becomes meaningful when thinking becomes active.

FROM KNOWING TO DOING

Learning should be experienced.

RizSim creates interactive learning experiences in which learners interpret situations, make decisions, receive feedback, reflect and apply ideas in new contexts.

Your authenticated experience adds the tools appropriate to your access while preserving one consistent RizSim environment.

Faculty, Course Leads, Institutions and platform administrators therefore work inside the same visual learning environment rather than being redirected into a separate website.
