About RizSim

Learning becomes meaningful when people act on it.

RizSim is an interactive learning platform built around decisions, reflection and application. Instead of treating learning as passive content consumption, RizSim places learners inside situations where they must interpret, decide, reconsider and apply.

WHAT IS RIZSIM?

Learning designed around action.

RizSim creates browser-based simulations and interactive learning experiences for education, executive learning, professional development and structured classroom activities.

The platform is designed to help move concepts beyond explanation. Learners encounter a situation, make a choice or judgment, engage with feedback or reflection, and then carry that learning into another context.

Faculty and institutions can bring RizSim experiences into their own teaching environments, while students participate through managed class access rather than purchasing individual classroom simulations themselves.

THE LEARNING APPROACH

A journey from judgment to transfer.

RizSim experiences are structured around learner action. The exact activity may differ, but the learning journey remains focused on meaningful decisions and application.

01

Explore

Learners encounter a situation, problem, stimulus or decision context.

02

Decide

Learners commit to a judgment instead of remaining passive observers.

03

Reflect

Feedback, comparison or structured reflection helps learners reconsider their reasoning.

04

Apply

Learning is transferred into another context so understanding can become usable judgment.

HOW ACCESS WORKS

Faculty lead the learning experience.

RizSim is designed around faculty- and institution-managed learning. Faculty license a simulation, create the learning session and share access with students through a class code or secure link.

03

04
Learning becomes visible Participation and permitted results return to the appropriate faculty environment.
