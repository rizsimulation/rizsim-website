## QR Code Generator for JavaScript

`rizsim-qr.js` contains qrcode-generator 1.4.4 by Kazuhiko Arase, followed by a small RizSim SVG wrapper. The original source copyright header is retained. The bundled encoder runs locally in the browser; RizSim does not send classroom links or share tokens to a QR-generation service.
