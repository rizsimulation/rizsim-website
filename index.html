How It Works

A learning journey built around action.

RizSim keeps the learner focused on decisions and reflection rather than passive completion.

1

Explore

Understand the situation and identify what matters.

2

Decide

Commit to a response, judgment or action.

3

Check

Receive structured feedback where appropriate.

4

Revise

Correct reasoning and see where thinking changed.

5

Apply

Transfer the idea to a fresh context.

Learning Gain

Track how reasoning changes during practice.

Where appropriate, RizSim preserves initial and final responses so instructors can see within-session correction rather than only a final score.

Research & Pedagogy

About RizSim

Closing the gap between understanding and application.

RizSim's core promise is simple: learning becomes more useful when people interpret, decide, receive feedback and apply.

Learning philosophy

RizSim supports active, decision-centered learning across education and professional development.

✓ Action before explanation where appropriate.
✓ Feedback designed to support correction.
✓ Separate subjective responses from learning checks.
✓ Browser-based experiences for learners and educators.
