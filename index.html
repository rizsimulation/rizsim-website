How It Works

A learning journey built around action.

RizSim keeps the learner focused on decisions and reflection rather than passive completion.

1

Explore

Read the context and identify what matters.

2

Decide

Commit to a response, judgment or action.

3

Check

Receive structured feedback when a defensible answer exists.

4

Revise

Correct reasoning and see where thinking changed.

5

Apply

Transfer the idea to a fresh decision or situation.

Learning Gain

Track how reasoning changes during practice.

Research & Pedagogy

Designed to make learning visible.

RizSim combines structured decisions, immediate feedback where appropriate, learning-transfer tasks and faculty analytics.

1

First-versus-final response capture

Preserves the learner's initial judgment before feedback and separates it from any corrected final response.

2

Scenario-level insight

Looks beyond one aggregate score to show where misconceptions, divergence or correction occurred.

3

Faculty-ready analysis

Authorized faculty can review class patterns and submitted responses through role-based Access OUT.

4

Transfer over recall

Learning experiences are built to move concepts into fresh decisions rather than reward memorization alone.

About RizSim

A platform built around the gap between understanding and application.

RizSim's core promise is simple: learning becomes more useful when people have to interpret, decide, receive feedback and apply what they know.

Our learning philosophy

RizSim supports active, decision-centered learning across education and professional development. Experiences can combine conceptual diagnosis, applied scenarios, learning gain, classroom analytics and facilitator debrief.

✓ Action before explanation where appropriate.
✓ Feedback that supports correction rather than simply revealing an answer.
✓ Clear distinction between subjective self-report and defensible learning checks.
✓ Responsive browser experiences for learners, faculty and facilitators.
